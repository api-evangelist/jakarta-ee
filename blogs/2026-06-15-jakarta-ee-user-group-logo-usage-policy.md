Effective as of June 15, 2026 The Eclipse Foundation welcomes and encourages the growth of independent Jakarta EE user groups worldwide. To support these community efforts, the Eclipse Foundation permits use of the official Jakarta EE User Group logo in accordance with this policy, the Eclipse Foundation Trademark Usage Policy, and the Jakarta EE Trademark Guidelines . 1.
